## 1. Which products are used, how popular they are, and where Russian-origin software stands

### Takeaway
The fiscal (ПРРО) layer is concentrated and moving to software. About 878 thousand PRRO were registered by May 2024, roughly 70% of all registered cash devices. Checkbox is the clear leader: it reports 220,000+ businesses and over 200 m UAH revenue in 2024. Вчасно.Каса, ПриватБанк's free «Каса», Cashalot and SmartKasa follow at a distance. For stock and back office, small shops use Торгсофт (one-time Windows licences), Poster (cloud subscription) and, historically, 1С/BAS. 1С/BAS are now sanctioned for the state sector, and a bill would extend the ban to private business. monobank's own PRRO domain now redirects to Checkbox. Checkbox's own back office, Posbox (launched 2025), is aimed squarely at small retail.

### Inferences
- The fiscal layer is close to a utility. Checkbox's 249 → 348 UAH increase (+40% in October 2026) and Вчасно's 249–349 UAH set the reference price small grocers already pay just to issue receipts. A POS that bundles Checkbox fiscalisation has to justify its price on top of that.
- Bank PRRO (ПриватБанк free; mono now funnelled to Checkbox) cover the cheapest fiscal-only segment. Neither offers stock, weighed goods or debts, which leaves room for a grocery-specific back office.
- 1С/BAS migration pressure is real for state bodies now and possible for private business if №13505 passes. Small shops on «BAS Роздрібна торгівля» are a plausible migration target, and Checkbox's Posbox is already chasing them.

## 2. Per-product support for weighed goods, scales, excise, customer debts, receiving, expiry, price tags, offline, and price

### Takeaway
Among the products checked, only the desktop/cloud back offices document scale and label-scale integration: Торгсофт as paid annual options, and Poster in its retail tariffs. None of the PRRO-first apps (Checkbox, Вчасно, Cashalot, Privat, the ДПС app) documents scale integration or parsing of scale-label barcodes; the ДПС app's weight is typed by hand. Excise-stamp entry in receipts is a legal must for alcohol and is supported at least by Вчасно's app. Customer debts are explicit only in Торгсофт. Offline is now standard everywhere, capped by law at 36 hours in a row and 168 hours a month.

- **Offline/blackout**: Kasa Manager works "автономно … не більше 36 годин підряд – відповідно до законодавства"; the Android beta also works offline. "2000 офлайн-кодів"; offline is cash-only, and receipts are fiscalised once the connection returns. — [Checkbox blog 2025-10-10](https://checkbox.ua/blog/blekaut-vedit-biznes-i-vydavajte-cheky-bez-turbot/)
- **Cash rounding**: the iOS app added rounding to 10 or 50 kopecks for cash payments, "особливо зручно для роздрібної торгівлі, де готівкові розрахунки це щоденна рутина". — [Checkbox blog 2026-07-02](https://checkbox.ua/blog/shcho-novogo-v-checkbox-za-cherven-2026/)
- **Pay later / pay in parts**: Checkbox issues prepayment and post-payment receipt chains. The rule: «Підставою для фіскалізації є факт отримання коштів — а не момент відвантаження». The final receipt must list the full items with the earlier advance shown as minus (ДПС clarification of 24 Oct 2023). — [Checkbox blog 2026-06-22](https://checkbox.ua/blog/yak-fiskalizuvaty-peredplatu-pislyaplatu/)

**Вчасно.Каса** [P]: the app has excise-stamp code entry, a camera barcode scanner, and Х- and Z-reports. Stock and scales are handled through the integrated accounting systems (1С handlers, API), not natively. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ua.vchasno.kasa&hl=uk), [kasa.vchasno.com.ua/torhivlya](https://kasa.vchasno.com.ua/torhivlya)

**Cashalot** [P]: VAT and excise calculation, offline mode, "Під'єднання торгового обладнання". The stock module adds receiving, inventory counts, auto write-off, transfers and suppliers. — [cashalot.ua](https://cashalot.ua/)

**SmartKasa** [P]: basic stock on every tariff, a built-in scanner (Business Plus), and Telegram-bot recognition of supplier invoices (testing). — [smartkasa.ua](https://www.smartkasa.ua/)

**ДПС «ПРРО Каса»** [S]: no scales; weight is entered manually as a fraction. — [DTKT](https://news.dtkt.ua/state/cash-handling/115174-elektronni-vagi-ta-prro-iak-zaznacati-kilkist-tovaru-v-ceku)

**Legal rules for weighed goods and price tags**
- Weight goes into "кількість товару" as a decimal (1.457 kg). [S, ДПС] — [DTKT](https://news.dtkt.ua/state/cash-handling/99037-vikoristannia-elektronnix-vag-iak-zapovniuvati-fiskalni-ceki-v-prro)
- Price tags follow Instruction №2 (order of 04.01.1997, "чинна станом на 2026 рік"):
  - weighed goods: name, grade, "ціна за 1 кг або 100 г";
  - pre-packed goods: name, grade, "ціна за 1 кг або 100 г", "фактична вага", "підсумкова вартість";
  - bottled and piece goods: weight or volume plus price per unit.
  
  [S, vendor legal explainer dated 2025-12-20, updated 2026-10-22] — [Торгсофт](https://torgsoft.ua/articles/law/tsinnyk-na-tovar/)

**Excise rules (alcohol, tobacco)**
- Since 01.10.2021, every RRO/PRRO receipt for alcohol must show the digital value of the excise-stamp barcode, under Мінфін order №329 of 08.06.2021. Excise goods may not be sold under generic names ("горілка", "сигарети"); each receipt line needs the exact product name, УКТ ЗЕД code, price and quantity. Stamp data are scanned, or typed if the equipment cannot scan. For gift sets, each bottle's stamp goes on the receipt separately. [S: ДПС Черкаська via sud.ua, 2026-04-08] — [sud.ua](https://sud.ua/uk/news/ukraine/357939-alkogol-v-podarochnykh-naborakh-kak-otrazhat-aktsiznye-marki-v-chekakh-rro)
- From 1 March 2025, Положення №13 requires the stamp's unique identifier in receipt line 9, or the identifier of an electronic stamp; "з 1 січня 2026 року очікується повний перехід на електронні марки". Fines: a missing УКТ ЗЕД code costs 5,100 UAH; a missing stamp costs 100% of the goods' value (150% repeat). [S, 2025-08-27] — [7eminar](https://7eminar.ua/news/11616-pidakcizna-produkciya-v-cekax-rro-yak-ne-pomilitisya)
- Alcohol sold by the glass: scan the stamp once, when the bottle is first opened (ІПК ДПС 18.07.2025 №3874/ІПК/16-31-07-05-11). [S] — [7eminar](https://7eminar.ua/news/10277-prodaz-alkogolyu-na-rozliv-cerez-prro-koli-ta-yak-skanuvati)
- еАкциз timeline per RAU partner content: test mode from March 2025; mandatory "для всіх виробників, імпортерів і продавців підакцизних товарів" from January 2026; reduced fines of 10–30% until the end of 2026. Retailers must track alcohol "від приймання на склад до продажу", including stamp checks on receipt and write-off of stamps on sale. **The two articles cite different law numbers (№3173-IX vs №3817-IX).** [S, advertorials by Consulting for Retail] — [RAU 1](https://rau.ua/news/etapi-vprovadzhennja-eakciz/), [RAU 2](https://rau.ua/news/eakciz/)

### Inferences
- Weighed goods are the clearest gap among the subscription PRRO apps checked: none documents scale integration or parsing of label-scale barcodes (prefix 2x). That is the main grocery differentiator. Торгсофт has it, but on a Windows desktop and as two separate annual per-device options on top of the licence: 3,790 UAH a year for each till with a scale and 3,870 UAH a year for each packing/label station, ≈7,660 UAH a year for a shop with one of each. Poster has scale integration, but its Starter plan caps the catalogue at 500 items, too few for a typical minimarket.
- A grocery vertical needs three weight paths: (a) a scale at the till via serial, USB or HID; (b) an export of the PLU list to label scales (CAS CL-Works style, ≤10,000 PLU); (c) parsing weight/price barcodes from label stickers. Weight also has to be carried into ПРРО as a fractional quantity. This matters for our own stack: CLAUDE.md notes `quantity` is INTEGER counted in `unit` (250 g = 250), which is compatible as long as the ПРРО mapping converts to kg.
- Excise: the till must capture a stamp code per unit, not per line, for alcohol, handle gift sets, and soon also e-stamp identifiers. Self-checkout cannot finish an alcohol sale without staff (see §5).

### Gaps
- Whether Checkbox, Вчасно or Cashalot natively parse weight barcodes or talk to till scales: no evidence found either way, and their help wikis were not reachable.
- Expiry-date tracking: only Торгсофт mentions it (via certificates). Its "термін придатності" article was not read; others are unknown.
- Excise for tobacco: whether stamp data must appear in the receipt (the line-9 requirement cites alcohol) is not verified.
- еАкциз: the actual 2026 status (in force or postponed), the correct law number and what a small retailer's POS must transmit are not verified at primary sources (ДПС, zakon.rada.gov.ua).

## 3. How a typical small grocery owner runs the shop day to day

### Takeaway
Direct evidence from small grocers is thin; I found no forum or Facebook threads readable. What exists shows owners running stock and debts in spreadsheets, notebooks and message history. Suppliers deliver on credit. Cash is still daily routine, alongside mandatory card acceptance. Blackouts and staff shortages are constant pressures, and owners prefer no subscription and simple tools over 1С.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor framing of the status quo: Posbox is for business "якому хочеться торгувати без ексельок та зошитів на колінці". [VC, 2026] — [Checkbox blog](https://checkbox.ua/blog/posbox-zruchna-alternatyva-1c-ta-bas/)
- Excel practice as Торгсофт describes it (Sept 2026): "Для невеликого магазину, де облік веде одна людина … таблиця може залишатися зручним інструментом". Typical failures: «Заборгованість доводиться відновлювати з листування»; «продавець записав повернення у своєму файлі, а власник сформував замовлення постачальнику за попередньою копією залишків». [S/VC] — [Торгсофт](https://torgsoft.ua/articles/stati/excel-migration/)
- A grocery owner's own words (Денис Іов, «Ібіца», a convenience store) **(2021)**:
  - «Магазин продуктів біля будинку "Ібіца" я розвиваю вже 7 років»
  - «Наш магазин продає: пельмені, ковбасу, заморожені товари, алкогольні та тютюнові вироби … десь між "Сільпо" та "АТБ"»
  - Most buyers are regulars from the surrounding blocks, two dormitories, a hospital, a maternity ward and a school.
  - On suppliers: «можна прийняти товар без оплати … часто привозять непотрібні позиції»
  - On choosing software: «мене привабила відсутність абонплати … 1С … дуже громіздко»
  - On staff: «зараз є великі проблеми з людським ресурсом»
  
  [P quote in a vendor case] — [Торгсофт «Ібіца»](https://torgsoft.ua/about/clients/produktovyij-magazin-ibicza/)
- Supplier documents arrive as photos and messages, and tools now try to read them: SmartKasa's Telegram bot for "розпізнавання накладних" (testing) [P] — [smartkasa.ua](https://www.smartkasa.ua/); Poster's "Створення постачань за фото" and "Імпорт постачань з Excel" [P] — [Poster](https://joinposter.com/ua/pricing-retail).
- Buying on credit means pay later: legally the receipt is issued when the money arrives, as a post-payment receipt. [S/VC] — [Checkbox blog](https://checkbox.ua/blog/yak-fiskalizuvaty-peredplatu-pislyaplatu/). Торгсофт offers "Реалізація товару в борг". [P] — [torgsoft.ua](https://torgsoft.ua/)
- **Card acceptance is mandatory**: Cabinet resolution (КМУ) №894 of 29 July (the page does not state the year; the 2023–2026 schedule implies 2022) phased it in from 1 Jan 2023 (towns over 25k people, removing the old exemption for shops under 20 m²), 1 Jan 2024 (5–25k) and 1 Jan 2025 (under 5k). Single-tax group-1 FOPs, vending machines, mobile trade and sellers of their own produce followed from 1 Jan 2026. Combat-zone and occupied areas are exempt. [S: RAU retelling NV] **(2022 rule)** — [RAU](https://rau.ua/news/bezgotivkovi-platezhi/)
- Paper and receipt costs (Checkbox at RAU Summit): "93% паперових чеків покупці викидають"; the tape for one receipt costs 30–40 kopecks; showing the receipt on screen saves "від 500 до 750 грн на місяць на кожній касі". Tap-to-Phone is pitched at "мікробізнесу". [VC] — [Checkbox blog](https://checkbox.ua/blog/vystup-checkbox-na-rau-summit/)
- **Blackouts** (chains, January 2026 during an arctic cold spell): outages of "10-12 годин поспіль". Varus says generator power costs "майже втричі" the grid tariff and burns 150 l of diesel an hour (large unit) or 10 l an hour (small). Файно Маркет (219 stores) saw operating costs up ~20%. КОЛО switched from generators to battery storage. [S, RAU interviews, 2026-02-05] — [RAU](https://rau.ua/news/zrostannia-vytrat-produktovogo-ryteyilu-cherez-blekaut-ta-chy-bude-podorozhchannia-tovariv/)
- Legal offline limits: 36 hours continuous and 168 hours a month (Checkbox, Торгсофт). — [Checkbox blog](https://checkbox.ua/blog/blekaut-vedit-biznes-i-vydavajte-cheky-bez-turbot/), [RAU/The Page](https://rau.ua/news/ogljad-prro/)
- **Staff**: vacancies for sellers and cashiers take 3–6 weeks to fill, and wage offers in retail rose 15–22% a year. [S, RAU, 2026-07-21] — [RAU](https://rau.ua/news/ryteyil-2026-chomu-prodavtci-staly-nayidefitcytnishoiu-profesiyeiu-i-iak-merezhi-z-tcym-boriutsia/). In food retail, "7-8 мереж платять працівникам менше як 10 000 грн при середній зарплаті по ринку більше ніж 20 000 грн" (Економічна експертна платформа). [S, 2026-06-29] — [RAU](https://rau.ua/news/spilnymy-zusylliamy-iak-derzhava-u-spivpratci-z-ryteyileramy-mozhe-systemno-vyvodyty-ekonomiku-z-tini/)
- Shadow economy around excise goods: the tobacco market's shadow share is ~17%. [S, same source] — [RAU](https://rau.ua/news/spilnymy-zusylliamy-iak-derzhava-u-spivpratci-z-ryteyileramy-mozhe-systemno-vyvodyty-ekonomiku-z-tini/)

### Inferences
- The everyday debt record is customer by name, running balance, partial payments. It lives in notebooks, spreadsheets or chat history, and the fiscal receipt comes only when money is paid. A grocery vertical should therefore keep debts as a non-fiscal ledger tied to a customer, and issue the ПРРО receipt on each repayment (pay later or pay in parts).
- Small shops must keep selling through 10–12 hour outages on a power bank or a small generator. That makes an offline desktop or tablet till (cash, scale, label printing) essential rather than optional, within the 36 h / 168 h legal caps.
- The distributor relationship (consignment, "прийняти без оплати", unwanted lines pushed by reps) suggests supplier-debt and returns-to-supplier flows matter as much as customer debts.

### Gaps
- No verbatim owner quotes about a «зошит боргів», Viber/Telegram ordering from suppliers or cash-only operation in 2024–2026: forum and Facebook content was not accessible and search was exhausted.
- No data on the cash vs card split in small food retail, and none on how many small shops actually use any software beyond the PRRO.

### Takeaway
Customers now meet self-checkouts in a third or more of transactions, Scan&Go in Varus, app-based payment and AI carts in Сільпо, app-first loyalty («Власний Рахунок» has 3 m+ active users) and self-weighing with a sticker in the hall at АТБ. ### Inferences
- Shoppers expect weighed produce to carry a printed label (weight, price per kg, total, barcode) whether they weigh it themselves (АТБ) or a seller does. For a small shop, a single label-printing scale at the produce or deli counter plus barcode parsing at the till gives the "chain" experience without self-checkouts.
- Loyalty in a small shop does not need an app: the chains' own research shows people forget programmes. The dominant pattern is "name the phone number at the till". A personal discount or bonus by phone number, already in our clothing vertical (C0), maps directly.

### Inferences
- Design priorities that follow from the complaints:
  1. Never block selling on КЕП or login: keep a local session and a cached signature flow.
  2. Close shifts automatically and reliably, with clear Z-reports.
  3. Make catalogue and stock editing work on a phone and a tablet.
  4. Make prices match between back office and till (a single source of truth, which our offline snapshot provides).
  5. Keep the offline transition seamless and instant, not a 1–5 minute wait.
  6. Have the scale fill in the weight.
- The Checkbox price rises are a churn moment. Pitching "POS plus fiscalisation in one price" against Checkbox at 348 UAH plus a back office could resonate, but our ПРРО also runs on Checkbox, so its tariff passes through. That needs a commercial decision.

### Gaps
- No systematic complaint data (e.g., rated review samples) for Checkbox's current app: its Play Store package was not found from the site. Torgsoft and Poster reviews were not collected.
- No owner complaints specifically about label scales (PLU upload failures, barcode format mismatches) were found; that would need forums or installers' Telegram channels, which were not accessible.
