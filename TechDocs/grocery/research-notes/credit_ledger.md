# Customer-credit ledgers («в борг», «під запис», udhar/khata, utang, fiado) — digital debt notebooks and POS tabs

Source tags used on every bullet: **[P]** primary (the vendor's own page, help centre or app listing; a law or official policy text); **[L]** local snapshot of a primary source kept in this repository; **[S]** second-hand (press, blog, aggregator); **[S-snippet]** seen only in a search-result summary — the page itself was not opened (paywall, 403, or the session's shared web-search budget ran out), so treat it as lower confidence. Research date 2026-10-22. The session's shared web-search budget was exhausted about two thirds of the way through; everything after that point comes from direct fetches of pages already identified, which is why the Ukrainian-practice section is thin (see its Gaps).

## 1. India — Khatabook, OkCredit, Vyapar: adoption, core flows, reminders, money, what happened later

### Takeaway
India is where the digital «зошит боргів» was proven at scale. Khatabook (8M+ merchants by May 2020) and OkCredit (claims 1 crore+ downloads, 2,800+ cities) won because of three things. Entering a debt is a two-field act (customer, amount). Every entry automatically texts the customer, which works both as confirmation and as a soft reminder. A reminder carries a UPI payment link that settles the khata by itself. The free ledger never paid for itself, though. Both companies spent years trying to monetise through payments, premium tiers and lending. Both cut staff (OkCredit 2022, Khatabook 2023). Both were still loss-making in their latest reported year (Khatabook FY24: ~₹116 Cr loss on ~₹103 Cr revenue; OkCredit FY25: ₹23.2 Cr loss on ₹23.3 Cr revenue).

### Cited Findings
**Khatabook: origin, scale, funding**
- Origin: in 2016 Vaibhav Kalpe, a graduate from Nanded, watched his shopkeeper father struggle to track customer credit and built a basic Android app. By late 2018 it had about 60,000 downloads, Ravish Naresh (a Housing.com co-founder) joined, and the app was relaunched after a revamp in December 2018 — [S, Ameya Deshmukh Substack, 11 Jan 2021](https://ameya.substack.com/p/how-khatabook-became-the-premier)
- May 2020: raised a $60M Series B led by B Capital, with Sequoia India, DST Global, Tencent, GGV and others; $87M raised in total; valuation $275–300M per a person familiar. The CEO claimed "more than 8 million merchants across over 700 districts" and about $200M of transactions added by merchants per day. AppAnnie (early May 2020) put daily active users at ~910k for Khatabook, ~520k for Paytm's merchant app, 352k for OkCredit, 231k for PhonePe and ~120k for BharatPe. Naresh: "We spent most of last year growing our user base." Online lending to merchants was named as a possible next product — [S, TechCrunch, 19 May 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/05/19/indias-khatabook-raises-60-million-to-help-merchants-digitize-bookkeeping-and-accept-payments-online)
- COVID shock: daily active users fell 50% in March–April 2020 and were back to about 90% of pre-pandemic levels after the June 2020 reopening. Growth came mainly by word of mouth. The same post's subtitle gives "2.5 million downloads / ~1M DAU / $300M valuation", which is inconsistent with TechCrunch's 8M merchants, so prefer TechCrunch — [S, Substack](https://ameya.substack.com/p/how-khatabook-became-the-premier)
- August 2021: $100M Series C led by Tribe Capital and Moore Strategic Ventures at "almost $600 million" valuation — [S-snippet, PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/?p=1138478). The company was described as having 10 million monthly active merchant users who "manage more than 344 million customers" on its platforms — [S-snippet, Business Standard topic page](https://www.business-standard.com/topic/khatabook)
- Features described in 2021: free SMS or WhatsApp updates on every transaction, automatic online backup, editing and deleting past entries, a PDF report of a customer's udhaar, reminder dates for customers to pay, several shops under one account, and access from several devices — [S, Substack](https://ameya.substack.com/p/how-khatabook-became-the-premier)

**Khatabook: money and later history**
- Monetisation was "a persistent problem" because the segment is largely cash-based and merchants prefer labour to technology. Vyapar, which monetised early (IndiaMart-backed), grew less — [S, Substack](https://ameya.substack.com/p/how-khatabook-became-the-premier)
- FY22 revenue from operations was Rs 71.1 crore (fourfold growth) against a Rs 111.1 crore loss. In October 2022 Naresh targeted a Rs 1,000 crore loan book by October 2023 and profitability in mid-2024 — [S-snippet, People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/strategic-hr/ms-dhoni-backed-khatabook-implements-layoffs-for-profitability-who-will-be-impacted-38905); [S-snippet, Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/khatabook-fires-over-40-employees-restructuring-exercise)
- September 2023: 42 employees (6% of about 700) laid off for "profitability goals", with a separation package of 3 months' pay. A spokesperson said revenue had grown more than 3x in 12 months and burn had been cut 50%. Total raised to date was $187M — [S-snippet, People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/strategic-hr/ms-dhoni-backed-khatabook-implements-layoffs-for-profitability-who-will-be-impacted-38905)
- FY24 (year to March 2024): revenue from operations ₹102.7 crore (+27% from ₹80.88 crore), net loss ₹116.24 crore (down from ₹125.43 crore), total expenses ₹230.08 crore. "Other expenses incl. contractor and payment gateway charges" rose 51% to ₹106.44 crore, consistent with a growing payments business. The company is described as a "digital bookkeeping and lending startup" — [S-snippet, YourStory, Nov 2024, from RoC filings](https://yourstory.com/2024/11/ms-dhoni-backed-khatabook-clocks-rs-1027-cr-revenue-cuts-losses-7-in-fy24). Conflicting: Inc42 lists FY24 total revenue at ₹113.8 crore with the same ₹116.2 crore loss — [S-snippet, Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/khatabook/financials/)
- Khatabook acts as a digital lending partner of Kinara Capital. Its grievance policy escalates support → customer success manager → nodal officer → RBI Ombudsman after 30 working days — [S-snippet, Kinara Capital](https://kinaracapital.com/?p=25468)

**OkCredit: product, from its own pages (2026)**

- Reminders: "Every outstanding balance triggers an automatic SMS reminder to your customer." One tap sends a WhatsApp reminder with a payment link. - Languages: 11 — English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi — [P, okcredit.in](https://okcredit.in/en/)
- Pricing: Basic is "Free, forever" with unlimited customers, entries, reminders and backup. Premium adds ad-free use, multi-device access, GST billing, unlimited SMS reminders and priority support — [P, okcredit.in](https://okcredit.in/en/)
- Claimed scale: "1 CRORE+" downloads, "Loved by 1 crore+ shopkeepers on Google Play", 4.6★ from 4 lakh+ ratings, 2,800+ cities — [P, okcredit.in](https://okcredit.in/en/)
- Kirana page: "Fast entry is the difference between a khata you keep and one you abandon during rush hour." It also promises a running balance per customer, month-end reminders to every customer at once, UPI payment straight from the reminder that will "settle against payments as they come in", and "The app works offline and syncs when the phone reconnects." — [P, okcredit.in kirana page](https://okcredit.in/khata-app-for-kirana-store)

**OkCredit: what happened later**
- February 2022: about 30–40 employees laid off over a month, with a stated refocus on "fintech initiatives" — [S-snippet, HRKatha](https://www.hrkatha.com/news/okcredit-lays-off-about-40-employees/). Entrackr's anonymous sources called it a realignment away from bookkeeping and e-commerce enablement: "The company used to have over 140 people but now it's about 80 people" — [S-snippet, Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/?p=127540). CEO Harsh Pokharna separately described letting 70 people go because of high burn from hiring too fast, with three months' notice (date unclear) — [S-snippet, People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/article/business/okcredit-ceo-harsh-pokharna-shows-the-right-way-to-handle-layoffs-45039)
- FY25: operating revenue INR 23.3 Cr (+64% from 14.2 Cr), net loss INR 23.2 Cr (−34% from 35.1 Cr), total expenses INR 48.5 Cr, employee costs INR 25 Cr, advertising down 83% to INR 83 lakh. Raised about $84M (Lightspeed, Tiger Global, Morningside, Y Combinator, Venture Highway). Founded 2017 by Aditya Prasad, Gaurav Kumar and Harsh Pokharna. The article says "over 1 Cr downloads on Google Play" but also "over 100 Mn downloads to date", so the figures conflict. Inc42 adds that both rivals are cutting losses "ahead of a potential consolidation phase" — [S, Inc42, 24 Oct 2025](https://inc42.com/buzz/tiger-global-backed-okcredit-narrows-fy25-loss-by-34-to-inr-23-cr/)

**Vyapar: credit features**
- Payment reminders are switched on in Settings → PARTY → "Payment Reminders", and each party needs a saved mobile number. The template uses the placeholders "[Party Name], [Amount], and [Business Name]" and the owner can append bank details or a UPI link. Reminders go per party via the WhatsApp/SMS icon, or in bulk via ⋮ → Payment Reminder → "Send Reminders in Bulk". Reminder SMS are provided free. All of these are user-triggered, despite "automated alerts" wording — [P, Vyapar guide](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-send-payment-reminders-in-vyapar)
- Credit limit: set per party in the "Credit & Balance" tab. When an invoice is created the app shows the available limit and current outstanding, and it warns when the limit is exceeded — [P/S-snippet, Vyapar video page](https://vyaparapp.in/videos/set-credit-limit-for-customer). Ageing buckets of 30/60/90+ days and upcoming-due alerts — [P/S-snippet, Vyapar AR page](https://vyaparapp.in/free/small-business-accounting-software/accounts-receivable)

### Inferences
- The SMS-per-entry design does three jobs at once: confirmation (the customer is told at the moment of the debt), evidence ("solid proof") and acquisition (every customer of every shop sees the brand). For a Ukrainian shop the same message is the cheapest answer to «а я стільки не брала».
- Removing the face-to-face ask is a selling point in its own right. The reminder is sent "by the app" with a pay link, and the payment closes the debt without the shopkeeper typing anything.
- Neither company monetised the ledger itself. Revenue came from payments, premium extras (multi-device, unlimited SMS, GST billing) and lending, and lending is where regulation and trust risk concentrate (§7). A POS that already charges a subscription does not need the ledger to pay its own way. It is a retention feature that ties regulars to the till.

### Gaps
- **Main-screen layout** could not be verified: Google Play and khatabook.com pages did not render for the fetcher. The following is a recollection to check on a device, **not a sourced fact**: Khatabook's home shows totals «You will get / You will give» over a customer list with red/green balances, and the entry screen has two buttons, "YOU GAVE ₹" and "YOU GOT ₹".
- Voice entry, a customer self-view web link in the SMS, attaching a photo of the bill or paper note, and dispute flows: none found in primary sources.
- Khatabook FY25 figures and its 2024–2026 status (profitability, lending book size): not found.
- India's legal regime for these messages (DPDP Act consent, TRAI DLT templates for SMS, RBI digital-lending rules on harassment and debt-shaming) was not researched, because the search budget ran out.

## 2. Indonesia and Southeast Asia — BukuWarung, BukuKas/Lummo, Philippine «utang», Vietnam, Thailand

### Takeaway
Indonesia copied India almost exactly. BukuWarung (6.5M registered merchants in 2021, 8M+ claimed now) survived by turning the debt book into a funnel for payments, PPOB top-ups, EDC devices and loans, and cut losses 75% in 2023. BukuKas, re-branded Lummo, raised about $147M, pivoted to an e-commerce store builder, and entered voluntary liquidation in September 2023. In Vietnam the ledger is mostly a feature inside general POS/sales apps (Sapo, MISA eShop and small app-store tools) rather than a standalone category. In the Philippines the money went to B2B supply and working-capital apps for sari-sari stores, not to customer-utang ledgers.

### Cited Findings
**BukuWarung**
- June 2021: claimed "more than 6.5 million registered merchants in 750 Indonesian cities", mostly in Tier 2/3 towns, and about $1.4B in annualised payments. Raised a $60M Series A led by Valar Ventures and Goodwater ($80M total; valuation estimated at $225–250M). It "began as a digital bookkeeping app", aimed to be an "operating system" for MSMEs, and planned lending, savings and insurance after experiments with bank and fintech partners. CEO Abhinay Peddisetty: "We don't see this space as a winner takes all" — [S, TechCrunch, 9 Jun 2021](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2163590). An earlier round cited 3.5M merchants — [S-snippet, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2052983)
- Why the reminder matters: the platform "bisa secara otomatis mengingatkan pelanggan" (can automatically remind customers), and the reminder message includes digital or non-cash payment options. Head of Product Mario Nicolas said many UMKM give credit, especially during the pandemic, and that at the micro level "banyak pelaku yang segan atau tidak enak ketika menagih" (many owners feel reluctant or embarrassed to collect). Users are dominated by "toko kelontong, penjual pakaian, warung, pedagang pulsa, sampai penjual kosmetik" (grocery kiosks, clothing sellers, warungs, airtime traders, cosmetics sellers) — [S-snippet, Bisnis.com, 10 Aug 2021](https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20210810/563/1428325/umkm-suka-ngutangin-pembeli-aplikasi-bukuwarung-bisa-bantu-jadi-pengingat-tagihan) (page returned 403 on fetch)
- The site today claims "8 Juta+ Juragan" (8M+ merchants) in 750+ locations across 34 provinces. Bookkeeping covers "hutang-piutang dengan mudah" (debts and receivables, easily). «Tagih» sends a payment request to the customer over WhatsApp with a payment link. Payments run over bank transfer, e-wallets, QRIS and retail outlets. «Produk Digital (PPOB)» covers pulsa, data, token listrik, e-wallet top-ups, PDAM water, BPJS and game vouchers. The EDC «BukuAgen» device and "Mini ATM" are promoted. «Solusi Modal Usaha» offers "Pinjaman modal usaha … tanpa agunan hingga Rp50 Juta" (collateral-free loans up to Rp50M, tenor up to 6 months, through OJK-supervised partners) — [P, bukuwarung.com](https://bukuwarung.com/)
- Finances (headlines only): 2021 net loss $19M; losses widened in 2022 despite a revenue jump; "slashes losses by 75% in 2023" (14 Aug 2024) — [S, DealStreetAsia headline](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/bukuwarung-earnings-2023-408041)

**BukuKas → Lummo (failure)**
- Founded in December 2019 as BukuKas, an app-based bookkeeping tool, by Krishnan Menon and Lorenzo Peracchione. In 2020 it launched TOKKO, an online D2C store builder later renamed LummoShop. Series C of $80M (2022) was led by Tiger Global and Sequoia Capital India with CapitalG and Jeff Bezos; about $147M raised in total. On 7 June 2022 about 50 Indian staff and 100–150 Indonesian staff were laid off and the entire Indonesian sales team dissolved; the company said only a "single digit number" in India were affected and that it remained committed to LummoSHOP — [S, Inc42, 8–9 Jun 2022](https://inc42.com/buzz/sequoia-capital-backed-lummo-lays-off-50-indian-workforce/)
- January 2021: a $10M round led by Sequoia India, with the stated goal of an "end-to-end software stack" for Indonesian SMEs — [S-snippet, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/01/11/bukukas-raises-10-million-led-by-sequoia-capital-india-to-build-a-end-to-end-software-stack-for-indonesian-smes/amp)
- Lummo was "weighing shutdown, to return over $70m to investors" — [S-snippet, DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/lummo-may-close-operations-340722). On 2 September 2023 it "enters voluntary liquidation, appoints Kroll as liquidator" — [S, DealStreetAsia headline](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/indonesia-lummo-liquidation-360554). An Indonesian academic paper concludes the new model "did not have the potential that had been expected" — [S-snippet, Unsoed journal](https://jos.unsoed.ac.id/index.php/jpba/article/view/9534)

**Philippines (sari-sari stores, «utang», «listahan»)**
- GrowSari served 50,000+ stores in 100+ Luzon municipalities out of 1.1M+ sari-sari stores (June 2021). Its app offers supplier ordering at distributor prices, telecom and utility bill payments, remittance, microfinance working-capital loans and "E-Lista", a seven-day loan. Customer utang is not mentioned — [S, TechCrunch, 21 Jun 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/21/growsari-a-b2b-platform-for-small-stores-in-the-philippines-adds-investors-like-temaseks-pavilion-capital-and-tencent)
- Dedicated utang apps found are small: SukliPH, "built for sari-sari store owners to track daily sales, utang, and inventory" — [S-snippet, hunted.space](https://www.hunted.space/product/sukliph); Tallyx, an open-source concept that describes utang kept "in notebooks or chat messages" — [S-snippet, GitHub](https://github.com/Polqt/tallyx). The bank-led e'Nay app (BPI BanKo) is for ordering and paying stock, not customer credit — [S-snippet, Unbox](https://unbox.ph/enterprise/bpi-banko-unveil-enay-app-for-sari-sari-store-owners/)

**Vietnam and Thailand**
- Sapo 365: "Ghi chép các khoản khách mua chịu theo từng đơn hàng, hóa đơn và sản phẩm" (records credit purchases by order, invoice and product). "Tự động gửi hóa đơn cùng lời nhắc thanh toán nợ qua Zalo, Messenger, SMS" (automatically sends the invoice with a debt reminder via Zalo, Messenger or SMS). Due dates can be set so "không quên nợ, mất nợ" (no debt is forgotten or lost). A confirmation goes to the customer after a payment is received, with per-customer receivable totals — [P, sapo.vn](https://www.sapo.vn/ung-dung-so-ghi-no-va-ban-hang-online.html)
- MISA eShop keeps debts per customer, aimed at retail and grocery shops, from 100,000đ/month — [S-snippet, misaeshop.vn](https://www.misaeshop.vn/12698/quan-ly-cong-no/). Small apps such as "Mua đây" (automatically accumulates each customer's debt) — [S-snippet, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/id6443977042) — and "SoDi" (auto payment reminders) — [S-snippet, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/sodi-qu%E1%BA%A3n-l%C3%BD-b%C3%A1n-h%C3%A0ng-pos/id6474116712)
- Pakistan (adjacent market): CreditBook, founded June 2020, raised a $1.5M seed (May 2021) and then an $11M round led by Tiger Global and Firstminute Capital (Dec 2021) for a bookkeeping app that digitises "the handwritten ledger" — [S-snippet, Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2021/05/04/karachi-based-creditbook-raises-bet-on-digitising-micro-entrepreneurs-with-1-5mn-in-seed-funding); [S-snippet, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/16/tiger-global-backs-fintech-creditbook-in-first-pakistan-investment/)

### Inferences
- The SEA arc confirms India's: the ledger is an acquisition engine, and survival depended on adjacent money flows (top-ups, bill payments, EDC, loans), not on the ledger. BukuKas died after leaving its core for e-commerce.
- In Vietnam the debt book is a feature of the sales app — debt created from the order, a reminder from the invoice, a confirmation after payment. That is the shape a POS-native implementation should take, as opposed to a separate notebook app.
- In every market the reminder channel is the dominant local messenger: WhatsApp in India and Indonesia, Zalo in Vietnam. For Ukraine the analogue would be Viber/Telegram/SMS (Ukrainian messenger shares were not researched here).

### Gaps
- BukuWarung's 2023–2025 revenue, loss amounts and active-user numbers are behind the DealStreetAsia paywall. Lummo's exact app shutdown date is not confirmed (a search summary said late May 2023).
- No Thai equivalent was found. Searches in Thai returned only generic apps.
- No Philippine customer-utang app with meaningful scale was found. Wikipedia's sari-sari article was not opened.

## 3. Africa and Latin America — Kippa, Bumpa, Kenya; «fiado» / «caderneta» / «cuaderno»

### Takeaway
In Nigeria, Kippa began as a bookkeeping/debt app and grew into payments and agency banking (500k+ merchants via POS terminals). In October 2023 it shut that agency-banking arm down and retreated to bookkeeping and business registration. Bumpa is an inventory/commerce app with no customer-debt feature on its homepage. In Latin America the paper «caderneta/cuaderno de fiado» is still widespread: there are interest-free 15/30-day terms, a copy of the booklet for the customer, and one or two defaults a month treated as a cost of business. The digital attempts are either small paid apps (Brazil's Fiado Fácil, priced by number of customers) or super-apps such as Colombia's Treinta, which moved toward payments, top-ups and B2B supply.

### Cited Findings
**Nigeria**
- Kippa "began as a bookkeeping business before adding payments products". It had raised about $11.6M across two disclosed rounds, reached "over 500,000 merchants across Nigeria's 774 local governments" through its POS terminals, and held a CBN Payment Solutions Services licence as a super-agent (cash withdrawals and deposits, account opening, bill payments). KippaPay launched in April 2023 and was shut on 15 November 2023, citing "profitable product portfolio consolidation". CEO Kennedy Ekezie: "This has been an incredibly difficult decision for us to make" — [S, The Condia, 18 Oct 2023](https://thecondia.com/kippa-layoff-kippapay-shutdown/)
- 40 employees laid off (last day 30 November). Kippa Start, business registration for ₦20,000, was also to close, and the company would "refocus on its bookkeeping and business registration offerings" — [S-snippet, TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/?p=121866). Techpoint put the affected core team at "70% of the workforce" — [S-snippet, Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/2023/10/19/nigerian-fintech-startup-kippa-to-shut-down-offline-payment-product-to-lay-off-employees-in-december/). The naira's post-June 2023 devaluation raised the cost of imported POS machines — [S-snippet, Marketing Edge](https://marketingedge.com.ng/nigerian-startup-kippa-to-exit-agency-banking-lays-off-40-employees/)
- Bumpa's homepage offers invoices, receipts, POS checkout, automatic stock updates and barcodes, at ₦5,000/month (Starter) or ₦10,000/month (Pro) billed quarterly, with a 14-day trial. It mentions no customer debts or credit sales, only "Record customer details with their purchase history" — [P, getbumpa.com](https://www.getbumpa.com/)

**Kenya**
- One search snippet describes a Kenyan village shop's debt book as a small notebook hidden "under a pile of biscuit boxes", holding the names of those who take goods on credit, with the entries becoming village gossip — [S-snippet, People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/?p=11207) (page returned 403; unverified)

**Colombia, Brazil**
- Treinta (Colombia), founded August 2020, had 5M+ users in 18 countries (about 1.2M in Colombia) as of July 2022 and a $46M Series A (April 2022). It offers sales and expense bookkeeping, a catalogue and online store, mobile recharges, and a card terminal at a 2.99% commission (device COP 120,000). The founders say about 95% of micro-business sales are in cash: "Queremos ser la súper app de referencia para los micronegocios de América Latina" (we want to be the reference super-app for LatAm micro-businesses). The article does not mention fiado — [S, La República, 16 Jul 2022](https://www.larepublica.co/finanzas/queremos-ser-la-super-app-de-referencia-para-los-micronegocios-de-america-latina-3404790). A Treinta marketplace credit lets tenderos buy stock and pay in 15 days "sin intereses ni recargos" (without interest or surcharges) — [S-snippet, Portafolio](https://www.portafolio.co/economia/finanzas/esta-es-la-nueva-apuesta-de-las-fintechs-en-colombia-para-sacar-adelante-la-economia-popular-616599)
- Fortaleza, Brazil (10 Jul 2022) — [S, Brasil de Fato](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2022/07/10/crise-economica-aumenta-venda-fiado-nas-periferias-do-ceara):
  - Francisco Holanda (30 years in business, about 30 credit customers in a paper notebook): «Todos os meses, levo um ou dois calotes.» (Every month I get stiffed once or twice.) On why he adds no interest: «O pessoal já ganha pouco e, se eu for cobrar a mais, o salário se acaba.» (People earn little; if I charge extra, their wages are gone.)
  - Gladson Ferreira (about 15 neighbours in a notebook): «Mas foi um calote pequeno, de mais ou menos uns 100 reais» (only one small default, about R$100).
  - Customer Elvis Marlon: «Eu sempre pago porque, se não, ele não vende, né?» (I always pay, because otherwise he won't sell to me.)
  - Economist Aécio Oliveira: «Geralmente os comerciantes incorporam alguns juros.» (Shopkeepers usually build some interest into prices.) The article says the total value of fiado is unmeasured because the practice is informal.
- Curitiba (2002, updated 2013) — [S, Tribuna do Paraná](https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/parana/armazens-conservam-o-fregues-de-caderno/amp/):
  - Valdeli Xavier Miranda keeps **two copies**: «Dou uma caderneta para os clientes onde anoto o que foi comprado e quanto foi gasto em determinada data.» (I give customers a booklet where I note what they bought and how much, on what date.) And: «Em um caderno que fica na mercearia, faço as mesmas anotações para não me esquecer das dívidas e poder cobrá-las» (In a notebook kept at the shop I make the same notes, so I don't forget the debts and can collect them.)
  - Terms: «Geralmente dou um prazo de quinze dias» (I usually give 15 days) — «para aposentados … aumento o prazo para trinta dias» (for pensioners I extend it to 30). On trouble: «Na maioria das vezes, parcelo as dívidas ou dou um outro prazo» (mostly I split the debt into instalments or give more time).
  - Ana Szpak: «Só vendo para quem eu conheço bem, tenho referências e sei que sempre paga.» (I only sell to people I know well and who always pay.) And: «Hoje, depois de levar muitos calotes, aprendi que isso não funciona mais.» (After many defaults I learned it doesn't work any more.)
  - Ronaldo de Camargo Lima: «Em cidade grande, acho que é quase impossível fazer isso, pois é muito difícil conhecer todo mundo.» (In a big city it's almost impossible, because you can't know everyone.)
- PagBank's advice to merchants (created March 2024, updated October 2026): pick who may buy on credit (an old customer is not always a good payer), keep a register (RG, CPF, full name, phone, e-mail, address), set a credit ceiling, set a clear due date, create a contact routine before the due date, renegotiate, take a deposit ("sinal") on larger purchases, collect "sem agressividade" (without aggression), and use a management tool (its PagVendas) — [P, PagBank blog](https://blog.pagbank.com.br/saiba-como-vender-fiado-com-seguranca-e-controle-no-seu-negocio)
- Fiado Fácil (Brazil): "Registre compras e vendas, gerencie parcelamentos e envie lançamentos diretamente para seus clientes pelo WhatsApp" (record purchases and sales, manage instalments and send entries straight to customers on WhatsApp), with due-date alerts, offline use ("Mesmo sem internet, continue trabalhando offline") and a web version. Plans are priced by **number of customers**: free up to 10, R$16.90 up to 35, R$25.90 up to 60, R$38.90 up to 100, R$64.90 unlimited. Rated 4.9★ from 208 ratings; contains ads; one reviewer asked to set the purchase date on manual entries — [P, App Store BR](https://apps.apple.com/BR/app/id6477870794)

### Inferences
- The Brazilian two-copy practice (customer booklet plus shop notebook) is the paper ancestor of OkCredit's SMS-per-entry and of a customer self-view link. Customers trust a tab when they hold their own copy.
- Shops already run a de facto policy with no software: who qualifies, a ceiling, a 15/30-day term with longer terms for pensioners, renegotiation into instalments, and no interest. A POS should encode exactly these knobs and nothing more elaborate.
- «Calote» (default) is accepted as a cost of business. The product's job is to shrink it through visibility and reminders, not to underwrite risk.
- Treinta and Kippa show the same gravity as India and Indonesia: ledger → payments/terminals/top-ups → lending/agency banking. Agency banking proved fragile — hardware import costs and currency shocks killed KippaPay.

### Gaps
- Kenyan digital ledger apps: none identified with a source (search budget ran out).
- Mexico and Argentina («fiado», «libreta», «cuaderno de fiados» apps, Tienda Pago): not researched.
- Kippa's current app features (debt tracking, reminders) are unverified, because kippa.africa returned 503.
- A Brazilian student poster reportedly claims "about 32% of sales are fiado" — [S-snippet, FEBRACE poster](https://virtual.febrace.org.br/2022/SOC/283/poster). The PDF could not be read, so the figure and its origin are unverified and should not be used.

## 4. US/EU POS — house accounts, customer accounts, store credit; «charge to account» as a tender

### Takeaway
Western POS vendors treat the tab as a **tender** ("House Account" / "Account"). It is tied to a customer with a spending limit and a lock or suspend switch, and it is settled by statements or invoices, often with an online pay link. Square's terms keep it from being lending — no loans, finance charges or instalments — and two products do not even let the balance be paid at the till (Square US, Vori). Square shipped house accounts only in August 2023. Lightspeed has the most complete model (limits, terms, statements, deposits, partial payments). Shopify POS offers **store credit** (a stored positive balance), not a charge-to-account tab. Clover has no native house account that could be found.

### Cited Findings
**Square House Accounts**
- Announced 28 August 2023 (post tagged AU, CA, IE, UK, US): a running balance for frequent customers, charged at the POS with the sale recorded and inventory updated, recurring invoices, card or ACH payment, spending limits, lock/unlock/archive — [P, Square Community product update](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Product-Updates/Square-house-accounts/bc-p/680508)
- US page: $0 monthly fee ("is free to use"); invoice payments at the normal rate (online from 2.9% + 30¢; ACH 1% with $1 minimum). "Currently, house accounts with outstanding balances cannot be paid at your point of sale." House accounts "do not store credit" — use a gift card for store credit — [P, Square US](https://squareup.com/us/en/house-accounts/retail)
- AU help article:
  - Accounts are created in the Dashboard: customer, location, spending limit, billing method; "the customer can make purchases within that limit".
  - At the POS: Charge → House Account → Confirm. No split tender with another payment type, and no Virtual Terminal.
  - "Locking" stops charges.
  - Balances are paid via invoices, which carry a PDF statement. Payments toward a balance are not counted as sales, to avoid double counting.
  - Prepayments cannot be collected. House accounts "can't be used to extend loans, impose finance charges, or accept instalment repayments".
  - Square notes that the seller carries the payment risk and advises consulting legal counsel.
  - [P, Square Help AU art. 8034](https://squareup.com/help/au/en/article/8034)
- Seller feedback (verbatim) — [P, Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Product-Updates/Square-house-accounts/bc-p/680508):

  - "I would love to use square as my main POS but house accounts are a major feature I need."
  - "the sale hits the day it is recorded and not weeks later when an invoice is paid!" (praise: accrual-correct sales date)

  - Requests for partial invoices and partial payments.
- A 2026 thread uses a house-account limit to cap a prize allowance; the reply relays that "Payments made to pay off balances aren't counted toward total sales to avoid double-counting" — [P, Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Payments-Troubleshooting/House-account-best-solution-for-credit-account/m-p/842129)

**Lightspeed Retail (R-Series)**
- Each account has a credit usage bar (limit, outstanding, overdue, available, deposit balance). Invoice statuses are Outstanding, Overdue and Settled. Setting the limit to 0 deactivates the account. Setting limits needs the "Customers – Credit Limit" permission.
- Payment terms: due on receipt, 15/30/60 days, custom up to 365, or a day of the month with a "buffer" — "Setting buffer time gives the customer advance warning about an upcoming invoice."
- Statements are emailed with a payment form (with Lightspeed Payments). "Partial payments must be made in person." Deposits work as credit memos (CR-001).
- [P, Lightspeed help](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/20656766930459-Managing-customer-credit-accounts)
- The "Account" tender works only if the available limit ≥ the sale total. In newer versions new customers default to a $0 on-account limit — [P/S-snippet, Lightspeed X-Series help](https://x-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/25533706500507). Statement settings cover payable-to name, days until due, percent due and "Display credit limit" — [P/S-snippet, Lightspeed](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/229129168-Configuring-customer-statements)

**Grocery and other POS (snippets of their help centres)**
- Vori (POS for independent grocers): "Store-managed credit lines let customers buy now and pay later". The account is chosen on the POS payment screen. Statuses are Active, Suspended (visible but cannot be charged) and Deactivated, and balances run negative to show debt. "You cannot pay a House Account balance through the POS… manually applied in Back Office" — [P/S-snippet, Vori help](https://help.vori.com/articles/10629974-house-accounts) (the fetch returned an empty page)

Yuma POS lets staff settle house-account bills at the terminal, and a purchase "cannot exceed the available credit" — [P/S-snippet, Yuma](https://support.yumapos.com/house-account-payments)

**Shopify and Clover**
- Shopify POS supports **store credit** as a payment method. Official article — [P, not opened, Shopify Help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/payment-management/store-credit/redeem-store-credit)
- Clover: no native house-account documentation was found. The only Clover hit was bar tabs on a higher Clover plan — [S-snippet, Lloyds Cardnet](https://www.lloydsbank.com/business/take-payments-with-cardnet/card-readers/clover.html)

### Inferences
- The tab is a **tender**, not an invoice. Square sellers' complaints show why: invoicing breaks inventory and the sale date. The sale should hit on the day it is made, and the repayment must not count as a second sale.
- Paying the tab off at the counter in cash is the main way village customers repay (see the Kyrgyz and Brazilian quotes). Square US and Vori forbid exactly that, so it is the gap to avoid. Lightspeed's "partial payments in person" and Yuma's pay-at-terminal are the models to copy.
- The common minimum set is: per-customer limit (0 = off), lock/suspend, balance on the customer card, an itemised statement, a repayment entry (full or partial), and a report that separates "sold on tab" from "repaid". Add-ons are terms with a due date, a buffer before the due date, and deposits/prepayments as negative balances. Square deliberately refuses prepayments, and Lightspeed allows them.
- Square's "no loans, no finance charges, no instalments" is a regulatory choice that a Ukrainian POS should mirror (§7).

### Gaps
- NRS (National Retail Solutions, the US bodega POS) and Clover native behaviour: no source found.
- The exact behaviour when a Square limit is hit (block vs warn) is not stated.
- Shopify B2B "payment terms" (net terms) were not researched.
- No EU-specific POS vendor (e.g., in Germany/Poland «Kundenkonto», «na zeszyt» in Polish shops) was researched.

## 5. UX patterns that made digital debt notebooks succeed — and the anti-patterns

### Takeaway
The products that replaced the paper notebook did not add bookkeeping. They made the notebook faster than paper, made the customer a witness to every line, and took over the awkward ask. The proven set is:
- entry in two fields
- a running balance per customer
- an automatic message per entry
- a reminder with a pay link that settles itself
- partial repayments
- a limit, a lock and a due date
- offline operation
- privacy between shop and customer

The anti-patterns are spam, exposure to third parties, public shaming, editing entries without trace, and moving debts around outside the system.

### Cited Findings
**Speed and form**
- "Fast entry is the difference between a khata you keep and one you abandon during rush hour"; "enter the amount and you're done"; customers come from phone contacts — [P, OkCredit kirana](https://okcredit.in/khata-app-for-kirana-store); [P, OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/)
- The paper benchmark: a Bishkek owner logged 28 people in half a working day — «Вчера за половину рабочего дня я записала в тетрадь 28 человек, которые взяли продуктов на 10 тысяч 295 сомов» (Yesterday in half a working day I wrote 28 people into the notebook who took 10,295 som of food) — [S, 24.kg, 3 May 2020](https://24.kg/obschestvo/151762_mnogo_prosyat_vdolg_kak_vyijivayut_nebolshie_magazinchiki_vmikrorayonah/amp/). Belarusian notebooks reduce an entry to a name or nickname and a number («Сашок 137 р»), and a crossed-out number means repaid — [S, Zerkalo, 14 Jan 2024](https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/58477.html)

**Running balance and statement**
- A per-customer running balance with payments settling against it — [P, OkCredit kirana](https://okcredit.in/khata-app-for-kirana-store). Lightspeed's credit-usage bar shows limit, outstanding, overdue, available and deposit — [P, Lightspeed](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/20656766930459-Managing-customer-credit-accounts). Square statements became "fully itemized" after seller requests — [P, Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Product-Updates/Square-house-accounts/bc-p/680508). Khatabook offers a PDF report of a customer's udhaar — [S, Substack](https://ameya.substack.com/p/how-khatabook-became-the-premier)

**Customer as witness**
- "SMS on every entry is solid proof", "automatic confirmation SMS", and customers need no app — [P, OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/). The paper version is a booklet given to the customer plus a shop copy — [S, Tribuna do Paraná](https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/parana/armazens-conservam-o-fregues-de-caderno/amp/). Sapo sends a confirmation after each payment — [P, Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/ung-dung-so-ghi-no-va-ban-hang-online.html)

**Reminders that remove the awkward ask**
- Automatic SMS reminders, a one-tap WhatsApp reminder with a pay link, a QR payment that "posts to their khata automatically", and month-end reminders to all customers at once — [P, OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/); [P, OkCredit kirana](https://okcredit.in/khata-app-for-kirana-store)
- "segan atau tidak enak ketika menagih" (reluctant or embarrassed to collect) is why BukuWarung's reminder succeeded — [S-snippet, Bisnis.com](https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20210810/563/1428325/umkm-suka-ngutangin-pembeli-aplikasi-bukuwarung-bisa-bantu-jadi-pengingat-tagihan). «Tagih» sends a WhatsApp payment request with a link — [P, BukuWarung](https://bukuwarung.com/)
- Vyapar's template ([Party Name], [Amount], [Business Name]) with an optional UPI link, and bulk sending — [P, Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-send-payment-reminders-in-vyapar). Lightspeed sends a "buffer" warning before the due date — [P, Lightspeed](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/20656766930459-Managing-customer-credit-accounts). PagBank: "crie uma rotina de contato com os clientes" (set a contact routine) and collect "sem agressividade" — [P, PagBank](https://blog.pagbank.com.br/saiba-como-vender-fiado-com-seguranca-e-controle-no-seu-negocio)

**Partial repayments and renegotiation**
- «Некоторые клиенты возвращают долг по частям, это очень радует.» (Some customers repay in parts, which is a joy.) — [S, 24.kg](https://24.kg/obschestvo/151762_mnogo_prosyat_vdolg_kak_vyijivayut_nebolshie_magazinchiki_vmikrorayonah/amp/)
- «parcelo as dívidas ou dou um outro prazo» (I split debts into instalments or give more time) — [S, Tribuna do Paraná](https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/parana/armazens-conservam-o-fregues-de-caderno/amp/)
- Lightspeed: "Partial payments must be made in person" — [P](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/20656766930459-Managing-customer-credit-accounts). Square sellers asked for partial invoices — [P, Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Product-Updates/Square-house-accounts/bc-p/680508)

**Limits, lock, due date**
- Vyapar warns at the limit — [P/S-snippet](https://vyaparapp.in/videos/set-credit-limit-for-customer). Lightspeed blocks the "Account" tender above the limit, and a limit of 0 deactivates the account — [P](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/20656766930459-Managing-customer-credit-accounts). Square has spending limits and lock — [P](https://squareup.com/help/au/en/article/8034)
- Paper practice: «Сейчас один покупатель может попросить продуктов на 1 тысячу сомов под запись» (now a customer may ask for 1,000 som on the tab), versus up to 15,000 before — [S, 24.kg](https://24.kg/obschestvo/151762_mnogo_prosyat_vdolg_kak_vyijivayut_nebolshie_magazinchiki_vmikrorayonah/amp/). In 2012 a Belarusian district committee capped essentials at 300,000 rubles per «вексель» — [S, Zerkalo](https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/58477.html)
- Terms: 15 days, 30 for pensioners — [S, Tribuna](https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/parana/armazens-conservam-o-fregues-de-caderno/amp/). Due dates in Sapo — [P](https://www.sapo.vn/ung-dung-so-ghi-no-va-ban-hang-online.html)

**Offline, devices, cost**
- OkCredit works offline and syncs later; online backup restores the ledger on a new phone — [P](https://okcredit.in/en/). Fiado Fácil works offline — [P](https://apps.apple.com/BR/app/id6477870794). Multi-device access is a paid feature in OkCredit Premium — [P](https://okcredit.in/en/)

**Anti-patterns and failure modes**
- Messaging rules: "Do not confuse, deceive, defraud, mislead, spam, or surprise people with your communications". Businesses need "opt-in permission from the recipient". Opt-outs must be respected "either on or off WhatsApp". "debt collection" is among the services that may not be promoted on the platform. The system "may limit the amount of messages a business can send" — [P, WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Privacy as a promise: "Entries stay between you and the customer concerned" — [P, OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/en/). The opposite: a village debt book becoming gossip — [S-snippet, People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/?p=11207) (unverified)
- Untraceable sums: Belarusian customers rely on the seller "not to add made-up amounts" — [S, Zerkalo](https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/58477.html). Commenters allege inflated sums in tetrad records — [S-snippet, charter97 comments](https://charter97.org/ru/news/2024/1/8/578667/comments/) (opinions, not facts)
- Debts moved between shops and staff personally on the hook. Convicted saleswoman Yuzina: «Соседний магазин отписывает долг мне, потом я этот долг да плюс свой — другому магазину» (the neighbouring shop writes its debt over to me, then I pass that plus my own to the next shop) and «И я оказалась последней» (and I ended up the last one). An Orsha saleswoman's notebook showed 29M of 107M (old rubles) unrecorded "gifts" — [S, Zerkalo](https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/58477.html)
- Leverage and coercion: some farm managers use unofficial guarantor lists as leverage over workers — [S, Zerkalo](https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/58477.html)
- Over-generosity as the failure: «Общительная и не грубая, все держала в чистоте, но слишком добрая» (sociable, polite, kept everything clean — but too kind), the village elder on the convicted saleswoman — [S, Zerkalo](https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/58477.html)

### Inferences
- **Recommended core for a Ukrainian grocery POS** (inference):
  1. A «В борг» tender on the till, available only with a chosen customer, and splittable with cash (a gap in Square).
  2. A per-customer balance shown when the customer is picked, with a limit (0 = off) and a lock.
  3. «Погасити» repayment at the till, full or partial.
  4. An optional message per entry and per repayment via SMS/Viber with the new balance, only with recorded consent.
  5. A due date and a reminder the shop triggers, single or bulk, with a pay link where the shop has QR payments.
  6. Offline entry on the desktop till.
  7. An itemised statement printable for the customer — the paper «книжечка» equivalent.
  8. Owner-only edits, with an audit trail: no silent edits or deletions; corrections are entries of their own.
- The «crossed-out = repaid» notebook habit maps to FIFO allocation of repayments to the oldest entries, with the statement showing what is still open.
- "Customer as witness" matters more in a village than reminders do. The Belarus court case (§7) shows that an unsigned seller's record may not prove the debt. A per-entry confirmation (SMS/Viber delivery, or a signature or PIN on the till's customer screen) is worth more than any analytics.
- Shaming lists, public boards or messaging anyone but the debtor should be impossible by design, not merely discouraged.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found for "photo of the paper notebook" import, voice entry, a customer self-view web link, or formal dispute flows in Khatabook/OkCredit/BukuWarung (app pages did not render). These remain unverified.
- No quantitative evidence was found that reminders raise repayment rates in small retail. A vendor blog's invoice statistic (78% vs 52% paid by day 15) is about B2B invoicing, came only via snippet, and should not be used.
- No studies of shame or harassment harms in shop-credit contexts were found; the evidence is anecdotal.

## 6. Business models and why players failed or shrank (cross-cutting)

### Takeaway
Free ledgers acquire merchants cheaply and virally (each entry messages a customer), but the ledger itself earns almost nothing. Every standalone player tried to monetise through payments, premium devices and extras, top-ups and bill payments, agency banking, or lending. The survivors are smaller, loss-making or only just break even. The failures came from abandoning the core for e-commerce (Lummo) or from capital-heavy agency banking hit by currency shocks (KippaPay). POS vendors earn from processing fees or subscriptions and offer the tab free.

### Cited Findings
- OkCredit: free basic tier; Premium adds multi-device, unlimited SMS, GST billing and ad-free use — [P](https://okcredit.in/en/). FY25 revenue INR 23.3 Cr against a 23.2 Cr loss; ad spend cut 83% — [S, Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/tiger-global-backed-okcredit-narrows-fy25-loss-by-34-to-inr-23-cr/)
- Khatabook: payments and lending. Rs 1,000 crore loan-book target for 2023 — [S-snippet, People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/strategic-hr/ms-dhoni-backed-khatabook-implements-layoffs-for-profitability-who-will-be-impacted-38905). FY24 revenue ₹102.7 Cr, loss ₹116 Cr, with payment-gateway-type costs rising — [S-snippet, YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2024/11/ms-dhoni-backed-khatabook-clocks-rs-1027-cr-revenue-cuts-losses-7-in-fy24)
- BukuWarung: PPOB, EDC/Mini-ATM, QRIS payments and loans up to Rp50M — [P](https://bukuwarung.com/). Losses cut 75% in 2023 — [S, headline](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/bukuwarung-earnings-2023-408041)
- Lummo: about $147M raised, a pivot to LummoShop, layoffs in 2022, liquidation in 2023 — [S, Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/sequoia-capital-backed-lummo-lays-off-50-indian-workforce/); [S, DealStreetAsia](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/indonesia-lummo-liquidation-360554)
- Kippa: agency banking and POS terminals shut in 2023, citing devaluation and imported-hardware costs, and a retreat to bookkeeping — [S, The Condia](https://thecondia.com/kippa-layoff-kippapay-shutdown/); [S-snippet, Marketing Edge](https://marketingedge.com.ng/nigerian-startup-kippa-to-exit-agency-banking-lays-off-40-employees/)
- Treinta: card terminal at a 2.99% commission, mobile recharges, B2B marketplace credit — [S, La República](https://www.larepublica.co/finanzas/queremos-ser-la-super-app-de-referencia-para-los-micronegocios-de-america-latina-3404790)
- Fiado Fácil: freemium priced by number of customers on tab (free ≤10, up to R$64.90 unlimited) — [P](https://apps.apple.com/BR/app/id6477870794)
- Square: house accounts free, with revenue from invoice processing (2.9% + 30¢ online; ACH 1%) — [P](https://squareup.com/us/en/house-accounts/retail)

### Inferences
- For a POS that already charges per store, the tab is a retention and differentiation feature, not a revenue line. Do not plan to monetise it through lending: that is where every standalone player burned capital and met regulation.
- SMS cost is a real constraint. OkCredit limits SMS in the free tier, and Vyapar subsidises it. A Ukrainian implementation should default to free channels (a printed slip, messengers the customer opted into) and treat SMS as metered.

### Gaps
- No unit economics (cost per SMS reminder, recovery uplift, churn) were published by any vendor found.
- Whether any 2024–2026 consolidation (OkCredit or Khatabook acquired or merged) happened was not found.

## 7. Legal and trust — privacy, consent, consumer-credit rules, fiscal receipts; evidence from Ukraine and Eastern Europe

### Takeaway
An interest-free shop tab stays outside most consumer-credit regimes: EU CCD 2008/48 excludes interest-free credit and amounts under €200, the UK exempts running accounts settled in one payment per period of ≤3 months, and Ukraine's consumer-credit law excludes interest-free *loans*. Charging interest, fees or instalments changes that, and Square's house-account terms forbid all three. In Ukraine the harder questions are elsewhere:
- **Fiscal.** A «розрахункова операція» is legally the *acceptance of money*, so a tab entry is not one by definition. Yet the ДПС document format has a «кредит» payment form, and ДПС has taken a strict "receipt at handover" line on deferred-payment deliveries. When the fiscal receipt must be issued for a tab is therefore unresolved and needs a ДПС consultation.
- **Evidence.** A Belarusian court refused to treat a saleswoman's notebook as proof of a 2,700-ruble debt.
- **Messaging consent.** WhatsApp requires opt-in and forbids promoting debt collection.

Direct sources on Ukrainian village «зошит боргів» practice were not obtained (see Gaps). The neighbouring Belarusian and Kyrgyz evidence shows the practice is alive and runs outside the cash register.

### Cited Findings
**Ukraine — fiscal law and the ПРРО data model**
- Закон 265/95-ВР, art. 2: «розрахункова операція» is «приймання від покупця готівкових коштів, платіжних карток, платіжних чеків, жетонів тощо» (accepting cash, cards, cheques, tokens etc. from the buyer) at the place of sale, plus refunds. A «розрахунковий документ» is a «документ встановленої форми та змісту» confirming a sale, return or service. Art. 3 obliges the seller to «проводити розрахункові операції на повну суму покупки» through a РРО/ПРРО and to issue a «розрахунковий документ … на повну суму проведеної операції». The visible text has no «кредит»/«відстрочка» wording; the only related term is «попередньо оплачених товарів». The current edition is 26.06.2026, and a further edition is scheduled for 01.11.2026 — [P, zakon.rada.gov.ua](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/265/95-%D0%B2%D1%80/print)

**Ukraine — civil and consumer-credit law**
- ЦК, ст. 694 «Продаж товару в кредит»: «Договором купівлі-продажу може бути передбачений продаж товару в кредит з відстроченням або з розстроченням платежу» (a sale may be on credit with deferred or instalment payment). Goods are sold «за цінами, що діють на день продажу» (at the prices of the day of sale). If the buyer is late, the seller may demand return of the unpaid goods, and «на прострочену суму нараховуються проценти відповідно до статті 536». «З моменту передання товару, проданого в кредит, і до його оплати продавцю належить право застави на цей товар» (from handover until payment the seller holds a pledge on the goods) — [P, ЦК ст. 694 via protocol.ua](https://protocol.ua/ua/tsivilniy_kodeks_ukraini_stattya_694/)
- Закон «Про споживче кредитування», ст. 3 ч. 2 п. 2: the law does not apply to «договори позики, що не передбачають сплати процентів чи будь-яких інших платежів» (loan agreements with no interest or any other payments). Short-term credit of up to three months is only partly carved out (ч. 3), small amounts are not excluded, and «відстрочка» is not mentioned — [P, ст. 3 via protocol.ua](https://protocol.ua/ua/pro_spogivche_kredituvannya_vid_15_11_2016_1734_viii_stattya_3/)

**Eastern Europe and Central Asia — the practice and its legal weakness**
- Belarus, «торговля на вексель». Goods, mostly food and sometimes alcohol, go on trust into an ordinary school notebook: date, item, price, or just «Сашок 137 р»; a crossed-out sum means repaid. Receipts are usually not issued «because the cash register must match the sales total». Per-customer limits are common. The practice is widespread outside the wealthiest areas, especially in Belkoopsoyuz village shops. Drivers named are low rural incomes, turnover targets, and perishables better sold on credit than binned. **Khoiniki district court (Gomel region)** ruled that a customer need not repay about 2,700 rubles because the saleswoman's journal was not an accounting document, so the shop had no proof; shortfalls may fall on staff personally. A saleswoman: «Да продавец и не станет связываться с пьяницей или ненадежным покупателем» (a seller won't deal with a drunk or unreliable buyer) — [S, Zerkalo, 14 Jan 2024](https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/58477.html)
- Russia, lawyers' Q&A: to recover a notebook debt you need the original notebook entry with the buyer's signature, a written demand and, if possible, the seller's testimony — [S-snippet, vseadvokaty.ru](https://vseadvokaty.ru/questions/grazhdanskoe-pravo/vzyskanie-dolgov-neustoyki-i-ubytkov/115160)
- Kyrgyzstan (2020 quarantine): «Мы рискуем, продолжая записывать их. Но тут уже зависит от совести.» (We take a risk writing them down; it comes down to conscience.) «Мы верим, что те, кто берет под запись, долги нам вернут.» (We believe those who take on the tab will repay.) «Здесь все друг друга знают, почти все соседи.» (Everyone knows each other here; almost all are neighbours.) — [S, 24.kg](https://24.kg/obschestvo/151762_mnogo_prosyat_vdolg_kak_vyijivayut_nebolshie_magazinchiki_vmikrorayonah/amp/)

### Inferences
- **Fiscal model (needs confirmation from ДПС/ЗІР or an accountant; inference only).** By the art. 2 definition, handing goods over without accepting money is not yet a «розрахункова операція». Accepting the repayment is, and must be fiscalised «на повну суму». That points to the following, not legal advice:
  - (A) at handover, print a **non-fiscal** tab slip («НЕ Є РОЗРАХУНКОВИМ ДОКУМЕНТОМ», like the existing pre-bill), and fiscalise the goods when they are paid for; or
  - (B) fiscalise at handover with payment form «кредит»/«ІНШЕ» if the ПРРО provider supports it.

  ДПС's strict stance on deliveries makes (B) the more defensive reading, and (A) the one that matches the definition. Which one ДПС accepts is the single biggest open question for this feature. The Belarusian «чек не пробивается, потому что касса должна совпадать» habit is precisely the non-compliance a POS must not automate.
- **Interest.** Keep the tab interest- and fee-free, with no instalment plans. That keeps it within the EU CCD art. 2(2)(f) logic (Ukrainian law follows the CCD) and out of consumer-credit licensing. Statutory interest under ЦК 536/694 exists anyway and is the court's business, not the till's.
- **Evidence.** Store who recorded each entry and when, its items and its price, plus the customer's confirmation (delivered message or signature/PIN). That turns the «зошит» from an unsigned seller's journal (rejected in Khoiniki) into a record the customer acknowledged.
- **Personal data.** The tab holds a named person's debt history, so restrict access by role (owner and cashier), never show balances on a customer-facing display without the customer present, and collect explicit consent for messages per channel.

## 8. Adjacent counter money features (brief)

### Takeaway
Wherever the debt notebook went digital, the same apps added counter money services: mobile top-ups and bill payments (PPOB), cash-in/cash-out through agent or EDC devices, and B2B supply credit for the shop itself. These earned more than the ledger but carried hardware, float and licensing risk (KippaPay closed in 2023). Loyalty for regulars was not a visible feature of these products.

### Inferences
- For a Ukrainian village shop the comparable adjacent services would be phone top-ups, utility payments and cash withdrawal at the till. Each needs a licensed payment partner, not POS code, so treat them as integrations to consider later, not part of the tab feature.
